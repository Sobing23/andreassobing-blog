Jeder Unternehmer weiß dass der Unternehmensfolg letzlich abhängig von ihm ist. Ein gutes Team zusammen stellen oder das Produkt zu entwerfen ist wichtig. Das wichtigste ist allerdings die Art der Führung.

Ob Unternehmer oder Teamleiter, hier sind 25 inspirierende Zitate zum Thema Führung.

1. "A leader takes people where they want to go. A great leader takes people where they don't necessarily want to go, but ought to be." – *Rosalynn Carter*
2. "Great leaders are almost always great simplifiers, who can cut through argument, debate, and doubt to offer a solution everybody can understand." – *Colin Powell*
3. “The first responsibility of a leader is to define reality. The last is to say thank you. In between, the leader is a servant.” - *Max DePree*
4. "If your actions inspire others to dream more, learn more, do more and become more, you are a leader." – *John Quincy Adams*
5. "Leadership is the art of getting someone else to do something you want done because he wants to do it." - *Dwight D. Eisenhower*
6. “Become the kind of leader that people would follow voluntarily; even if you had no title or position.” - *Brian Tracy*
7. "The leaders who offer blood, toil, tears and sweat always get more out of their followers than those who offer safety and a good time. When it comes to the pinch, human beings are heroic." – *George Orwell*
8. “I start each day by telling myself what a positive influence I am on this world.” - *Peter Daisyme*
9. “Earn your leadership every day." - *Michael Jordan*
10. “Leadership is lifting a person’s vision to high sights, the raising of a person’s performance to a higher standard, the building of a personality beyond its normal limitations.” - *Peter Drucker*
11. "My job is not to be easy on people. My job is to take these great people we have and to push them and make them even better." - *Steve Jobs*
12. “Great leaders find ways to connect with their people and help them fulfill their potential.” - Steven J. Stowell
13. "To have long-term success as a coach or in any position of leadership, you have to be obsessed in some way." - *Pat Riley*
14. "If you think you are leading and turn around to see no one following, then you are just taking a walk." – *Benjamin Hooks*
15. “The challenge of leadership is to be strong, but not rude; be kind, but not weak; be bold, but not bully; be thoughtful, but not lazy; be humble, but not timid; be proud, but not arrogant; have humor, but without folly.” - *Jim Rohn*
16. "A man who wants to lead the orchestra must turn his back on the crowd." - *Max Lucado*
17. "The ultimate measure of a man is not where he stands in moments of comfort, but where he stands at times of challenge and controversy." - *Martin Luther King, Jr.*
18. “No man will make a great leader who wants to do it all himself, or to get all the credit for doing it.”  - *Andrew Carnegie*
19. "Those who try to lead the people can only do so by following the mob." – *Oscar Wilde*
20. “Outstanding leaders go out of their way to boost the self-esteem of their personnel. If people believe in themselves, it’s amazing what they can accomplish.” - *Sam Walton*
21. “If your actions create a legacy that inspires others to dream more, learn more, do more and become more, then, you are an excellent leader.” - *Dolly Parton*
22. “In this world a man must either be an anvil or hammer.” -  *Henry W. Longfellow*
23. “You don’t have to hold a position in order to be a leader” - *Henry Ford*
24. "Don't blow off another's candle for it won't make yours shine brighter." *Jaachynma N.E. Agu*
25. “I cannot give you the formula for success, but I can give you the formula for failure, which is: Try to please everybody.”  - *Herbert Swope*
